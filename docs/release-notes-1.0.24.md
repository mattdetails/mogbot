# Mogbot 1.0.24

## What's new in 1.0.24

- **Chat bubbles you can tell apart** - Yours are solid, the agent's a quiet tint, with a small tail toward whoever spoke.
- **Catch up, calmer** - One row the height of a conversation, pinned under Search: who's waiting and how many, or All clear.
- **Agents | Projects full width** - The side-by-side button is gone from the sidebar; ⌘K still turns it on.
- **Folders that work** - Drag an agent into a folder, out of one, or between rows.
- **Plans cover the deploys they name** - One OK on a plan now covers redeploying that project for the hour.
- **Missions** - Give the team a goal with a share of your usage; it pauses at the share and asks.
- **The dev server starts when you pick an agent** - No more clicking Start again after coming back from Agents.
- **Usage says when a reading is from** - An old reading is marked "as of" instead of looking current.
