# Mogbot 1.1.1

## What's new in 1.1.1

- **Catch up, one calm page** - On the desk, Catch up is a page of its own: one centred column, the decision with its preview (who it goes to and from which account), progress as you go, and A, D, O and L to answer.
- **Pause one agent** - Pause an agent from its ⋯ menu and it stops picking up work: other agents' messages wait until you Resume it, routines skip, and your own messages still get through. A grey Paused tag shows on its row and in the chat.
- **Keyboard for everything** - ⌃⌥⇧1–9 opens a project, ⌃⌥C opens Catch up, ⌘↩ and ⌘⌫ answer a card; Help lists them all.
- **Recently deleted and Admins** - Agents you delete wait 30 days in Recently deleted, in the same card as Admins.
- **Message menu** - Hover a message for one quiet ⋯ with Reply, Copy, Rewind to here and more, in words.
- **Add a task yourself** - The tasks panel has Add a task at its foot.

## Better

- **What's in flight** - Reads cleanly on narrow windows and the header lines up with the page.
- **File paths in replies** - Every path is one chip; a missing one is struck through, on desk and phone alike.
- **The phone keeps up** - The screen you're on loads first; chats it reads ahead wait their turn.
- **Lighter preview glow** - The glow while an agent works in your preview no longer repaints the whole page.

## Fixed

- **Big files to the phone** - Files over 3 MB reach the phone, and a download cut short says how far it got.
- **Photos from the phone** - Pictures sent from the phone's library arrive whole.
- **The wrong account on this Mac** - Opening the app from another agent's shell no longer swaps which Claude account "This Mac" is.
- **Scrolling a chat on the phone** - No longer starts a swipe to reply.
- **Turns that stopped midway** - Fixed.
