# Mogbot 1.0.23

## What's new in 1.0.23

- **Unleashed only asks about money** - At Unleashed, agents just do the job: sends, posts, pushes, installs and deletes go ahead, even after reading a web page, and a plan of steps runs with a line saying what it did. Only spending money asks.
- **One OK per send** - With "exact words" on, the one card showing a post's or email's words covers every step that gets it out on that site, with no second card for the click.
- **Your phone stays calm** - Short drops in the link to your Mac are silent: nothing greys out or flickers, and the phone reconnects on its own. You only see "Can't reach your Mac" after a few minutes of trying.
- **Fewer drops in the first place** - The Mac notices a dead link in seconds and picks the conversation back up where it left off, so your phone doesn't lose its place.
- **Draft cards** - An email or Slack message an agent wants to send as you shows To, CC, Subject and the exact words, with Edit, Discard, Send and Draft in Gmail.
- **Catch up** - Go through what needs you one decision at a time, on the desk and the phone.
- **Calmer chat** - Agent replies sit in their own bubbles, a queued message says just "Next", and pages are pinned only when you ask.
- **No limit on copies** - Agents can work in as many copies of a project as they need; only a nearly full disk stops a new one.
- **Your Chrome, every time** - Agents reach your signed-in sites through Mogbot for Chrome, and Settings → Connected shows every way in.
- **Result cards keep their files** - A finished piece of work always arrives with its files attached.
