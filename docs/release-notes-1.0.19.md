# Mogbot 1.0.19

## What's new in 1.0.19

- **Your Chrome is the only browser** - Agents no longer open a browser of their own. They work in your Chrome, where you're already signed in, and if it isn't connected yet they ask you to connect it.
- **Progress you can follow** - The short "On it…" and "Found it…" lines agents post while they work stay in the chat instead of folding away into the timeline.
- **Adding a Claude account** - The sign-in now says it opens a new, empty Chrome window, so you sign in there with the account you're adding and your usual Chrome stays as it was.
