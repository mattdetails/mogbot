# Mogbot 1.1.0

## New

- **Reply to a message** - Hover a message and press Reply (or R), or swipe right on the phone; your agent sees exactly what you answered.
- **A task list for every agent** - Under each agent's name, a line says what it's doing and how many tasks wait; open it from the line or from Tasks in the ⋯ menu.
- **Agents hand each other work** - An agent can put a task on a teammate's list, and the teammate can hand it back with a reason.
- **Catch up, side by side** - What waits for you is on the left and the open item on the right, with A, D, O and L to answer without the mouse.
- **New agent in one screen** - Pick a name, a colour and a face (34 now) or an icon, add a sentence if you like, and press Create.
- **One menu per agent** - Usage, Routines and Model moved from the chat header into the agent's ⋯ menu, next to Tasks.
- **A new sidebar** (pending) - Rounded rows with when each agent last moved and an unread dot, and a gear at the foot for Settings, Routines and Pair your phone.
- **Room in empty folders** (pending) - An empty folder keeps a place to drop an agent, and says "Drop to move here" while you drag.

## Better

- **Chat that reads easier** - Your messages take your agent's colour, its replies arrive whole instead of word by word, and the message box has a + to attach and one round send button.
- **The phone holds on through drops** - A dropped connection now picks up where it left off, and a phone that changes network tries again at once.
- **Catch up on the phone matches the desk** - The same count, the same card and an "Up next" list under it.
- **Usage lines that don't crowd** - An older reading's "as of" sits under the figure instead of squeezing the account's name.
- **Approval cards say what Always covers** - Even on a paused card.

## Fixed

- **Replies that were only a list** - On the phone they no longer shrink to empty bullets.
- **An @name in your own message** - It's now readable against your message's colour.
- **Long drafts** - You can scroll them with the keyboard, and "didn't send" is easier to read in dark mode.
