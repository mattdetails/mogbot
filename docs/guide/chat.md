Every message is a bubble. On the phone, yours takes the agent's key colour, on the right, with white or near-black words, whichever reads better (at least 4.5 to 1); your agent's is a quiet tint of the text colour (4% in dark, 6% in light) on the left, up to 560 wide. A reply arrives whole, not word by word; the working line stays until all of it is there. Each has a small square corner at the bottom toward whoever spoke. Cards (results, approvals, tables and charts) keep their own frames and are not bubbles.

## When your agent is busy

## The message box

The box holds the paperclip, your words, the mic and Send, and nothing else. Send is neutral (near-black in light, near-white in dark), never the agent's colour, and sits faint until there is something to send. The mic is there only while the box is empty. While the agent works and the box is empty, a Stop square sits where Send would be; Stop stops the turn. The model is no longer in the box: change it from **Model** in the chat header (desk) or **Model and controls** in the ⋯ menu (phone).

Send something while it is working and it waits its turn. Under your message you see **Next**, and **Send now** if you'd rather it stop and read yours straight away. The agent's one working line sits below; that is all the chat says about it.

## A page above the chat

An agent can keep a page for a conversation, like a tracker or a status board. It only sits above the chat if you asked for that. Otherwise it is kept, and the **Page** button in the chat header pins it.

## Drafts to send

When an agent wants to send an email or a Slack reply as you, you get a draft card. Its title says who and where ("Email to Aurelie · Gmail", "Reply to Aurelie", "Reply in #beta · Slack"; Gmail is named only when the app knows your Gmail account). One block holds To, CC and Subject and the exact words, which scroll if they are long; under it, "From <account> · goes out as you" (just "goes out as you" when the app doesn't know the account) and "These exact words go out under your name." The buttons: **Discard** stops it, **Edit** changes the words first (for an email, also CC and Subject), **Draft in Gmail** opens your own Gmail with the draft filled in and sends nothing, and **Send** is your OK.

A Slack reply posts the words as you edited them. For an email, an edit goes back to your agent, which shows you the new version on a fresh card; what you approve is always exactly what is sent.

## Allow and Deny cards

A request's title says what will happen, in plain words, when the app can tell ("Delete ~/Exports/2024", "Install three@0.170.0", "Run the tests", "Push main to origin"); the raw command is one click away behind **Details**. When it can't tell, the card says "Run a command" and shows the command, as before. When one card covers a whole plan, the button says **Allow all 3** and a line under the list says what that one OK covers ("This one OK covers: send. Each step runs without asking again for an hour."). If the app has a reason for asking, one muted line says it. Once answered, a card folds to one line: its title and what you answered.

## Catch up

**Catch up** sits under Search in the Agents sidebar and stays put while the list scrolls: a row the height of a conversation that says how many agents need you and who, or "All clear" (the bell beside the sidebar toggle opens it too, and so does ⌘K). It shows one at a time: who asked and when, the request in a line, why in two, and, for anything that sends, the exact words. **Deny** and **Allow** sit under it (Allow is always black and white, never an agent's colour); **Open chat** opens that agent's chat; **Later** moves it to the back, and it comes back the next time you catch up. While a card has focus, A, D, O and L do those four; Esc closes. **Up next** lists what comes after, so it never feels endless. When nothing is left you see "You're caught up", and what only happened while you were away (a result, a routine's run), which needs no answer.

On your phone, Catch up is the same: one card at a time, the same Allow, and one last card for what only happened.

## While your agent works

One row at the end of the chat shows your agent's key and one phrase about what it is doing right now, in the app's own words for the step ("Reading Northwind's site", "In Chrome on linkedin.com", "Running the tests · 3 of 8", "Checking with Scout"). Before it has done anything the row says "Working on it". A timer shows after about 8 seconds. After a minute with nothing new it says "Still working" and the time. When it is waiting on you it says "Needs your OK on 12 emails" with a **Review** button that takes you to the card. The three dots appear only while a reply is being written.
