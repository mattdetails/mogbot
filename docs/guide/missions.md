A mission gives a goal a share of your usage. You say what you want, your Chief of Staff agrees with
you what counts as done, and your agents work on it until it's done, the share is used or the end
date comes, whichever is first. It runs on your Mac, so it doesn't run while your Mac sleeps.

## Starting one

Tell your Chief of Staff the goal. It asks a few short questions, then shows one card:

- **What counts as done**: two to five checks you can see for yourself, like "20 leads with a call
  booked".
- **Usage to spend**: a share of each engine's week, always "about", like "about 30% of Claude
  Code". An engine that doesn't report usage is capped by time instead.
- **Asks you first**: your own rules apply. Spending money always asks.
- **Updates**: when it's blocked, at each milestone, or daily at a time you choose.

Say yes and the mission starts. Open it from **What's in flight → Missions**.

## Setting one up yourself

Usually your Chief of Staff drafts the card from its questions. You can also do it by hand: press ⌘K
and choose **Start a mission…** (or **+ New mission** under Missions in What's in flight, or **New
mission** on your phone, one question to a screen). **Start mission** stays off until there is a goal,
at least one thing that counts as done and a share, and the line under the buttons says which is
missing. If a chosen engine hasn't reported its usage yet, you're asked once, in the dialog: "Codex
hasn't reported its usage yet, so its share is about 40% once it's read. Start anyway?".
**Save for later** keeps a draft in the Missions list, labelled "Draft" with its date; open it to carry
on with the fields filled.

## The page

The page shows the goal and its day ("Day 2", or "Day 2 of about 3" once there are two readings and 15% is done; it says "running longer than expected" if it passes the estimate), one number for progress, how much of each share is used, what waits on
you, each criterion, who is on it and what happened. A banner appears only for state: amber when it
paused at its share, red when something it needs is broken, neutral when it's ready for your review.

## While your Mac sleeps

Missions run on your Mac, so they stop while it sleeps. The page says "Paused while your Mac sleeps"
and, from your phone, shows the last thing it read rather than looking as if it were running. When
the Mac wakes it carries on, adds a line saying how long it was asleep, and a time cap only counts the
time it was awake.

## If something it needs breaks

A red banner says what broke and who it affects, that your usage isn't being spent, and offers
**Reconnect** for that app (it asks your Chief of Staff for the sign-in card) and **Pause mission**.

## On your phone

**Missions** appears at the top of the Agents list while one is open. It shows the same page in one
column: the count, each share worded "about", what waits on you with a Review button, each
criterion and who is on it, and Pause, Add 10% or Accept when those apply.

## Stopping at the share

Usage is read about every ten minutes, so a share is soft: a step that's running finishes first and
the mission may go a little over. Then it pauses, says where it got to, and asks: **Add 10%**,
**Finish with the other engine** or **Stop here**. It never keeps going on its own and never spends
money.

## Review

When the criteria look met, a separate check reads the work against them, on a different engine
where you have one ("Checked by Codex"). Each criterion comes back Met, Not met or Couldn't check.
You decide: **Accept**, **Keep going with 10% more**, or **See the work**. The agents never mark it
done themselves.
