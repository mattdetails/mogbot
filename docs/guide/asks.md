Every agent keeps its own list of what it has been asked, so nothing is forgotten when it gets busy.
When your Chief of Staff gives an agent work, or you ask for something an agent can't finish in one
reply, it goes on that agent's list until it is done.

## Where to see it

Open **What's in flight**. **Team tasks** lists each agent with something open: its first asks, how
many are open and how long the oldest has waited. Press a row to open that agent's chat.

- **Busy** means ten or more lines to do, or one open over a day with no movement. Give it less, or
  ask the Chief to move some.
- **Stuck** means an ask sat open after two reminders, or the agent hit an error on it. Your Chief
  of Staff says so in its chat.

Each line is Doing (one at a time), Next, Waits on you or Done. An agent can't remove a line without
ending it Done or Dropped, with a reason.

## What the Chief of Staff does with it

Your Chief of Staff knows what each agent is for and what each has open. It hands work to the agent
that owns that job, tells you when another agent already has the same thing, and gives less to an
agent that is overloaded. An ask that sits for a few hours gets a reminder, at most once a day and twice in all, then the
Chief tells you.

Reminders don't go out in quiet hours, in "Save my limits", while you are driving an agent yourself
(Take over) or while it is busy.

## Agents ask instead of guessing

Before work that needs something only you have (a figure, which client, a login, a choice), an agent
checks what it already knows, then asks you one specific question. It doesn't fill the gap with
something plausible, and it says so when it goes ahead on an assumption.
