Every agent keeps its own list of what it has been asked, so nothing is forgotten when it gets busy.
When your Chief of Staff gives an agent work, or you ask for something an agent can't finish in one
reply, it goes on that agent's list until it is done.

## Teammates can hand each other work

An agent can put a line on a teammate's list ("Send Globex's invoice to Mail"). It lands in the
teammate's **Next** marked "from" the agent that assigned it, and the teammate's chat shows a
centred line saying so. It doesn't wake the teammate; they pick it up on their next turn. Up to five
lines can be open on one teammate.

An assigned line stays open until the assignee marks it done or hands it back. Handing back always
comes with a reason ("Globex isn't a client of ours"), and the reason goes to whoever assigned it as
a message. It never just disappears. Lines you add yourself have no one to hand back to.

## Your own list

You can add to an agent's list yourself. On the desk, open its tasks panel and press **Add a task** at
the foot: the row becomes one field, **Enter** puts the line in **Next** in your own words, **Esc**
cancels. On the phone it is in the **⋯** at the top of the tasks sheet, with **Reorder** (move a
line one place up or down in its group) and **Drop a task**. A dropped line is closed with a reason
("Dropped by you", or the one you give) and stays in the list, muted, for the day. All of this needs
a computer running a recent enough Mogbot; an older one hides the rows.

## What you ask isn't lost

After a turn that began with your own words, the app reads what was asked and how the turn ended. If
the agent missed something you asked for, it is added to the list with a short title of its own; if
the agent ended on a question, the line it concerns moves to **Waits on you**. It only ever adds
lines and parks them: it never marks anything Done or Dropped, that is the agent's call (or yours).
It doesn't run for routines or for turns answering outside text, and it runs at most 30 times a day
per agent. It makes one short call to the agent's own engine each time, so it counts a little against
your plan.

## Where to see it

Open **What's in flight**. **Team tasks** lists each agent with something open: its first asks, how
many are open and how long the oldest has waited. Press a row to open that agent's chat.

- **Busy** means ten or more lines to do, or one open over a day with no movement. Give it less, or
  ask the Chief to move some.
- **Stuck** means an ask sat open after two reminders, or the agent hit an error on it. Your Chief
  of Staff says so in its chat.

On the phone, open an agent's sheet (tap its name in the chat) and **Open asks** lists what it has
open: each line's title and whether it is Doing, Next, Waiting (and on whom) or Stuck. It is read
from your computer, so it only appears when the app there is new enough.

Each line is Doing (one at a time), Next, Waits on you or Done. An agent can't remove a line without
ending it Done or Dropped, with a reason.

## What the Chief of Staff does with it

Your Chief of Staff knows what each agent is for and what each has open. It hands work to the agent
that owns that job, tells you when another agent already has the same thing, and gives less to an
agent that is overloaded. An ask that sits for a few hours gets a reminder, at most once a day and twice in all, then the
Chief tells you.

Reminders don't go out in quiet hours, in "Save my limits", while you are driving an agent yourself
(Take over) or while it is busy.

## Agents ask instead of guessing

Before work that needs something only you have (a figure, which client, a login, a choice), an agent
checks what it already knows, then asks you one specific question. It doesn't fill the gap with
something plausible, and it says so when it goes ahead on an assumption.
