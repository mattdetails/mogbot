The quickest way to teach your team is to talk. Your Chief of Staff asks one short question at a
time, in the chat or out loud with Talk on your phone, and writes down what it heard.

## Interview me

Press ⌘K and choose **Interview me…**, or say "interview me" to your Chief of Staff. It asks up to
six questions: what you do and for whom, what eats your week, the tools and people involved, and
what you'd hand off first. It ends with a few lines of what it heard and asks if that's right. On
yes it keeps it in its memory and suggests the agents that fit. If it knows nothing about your work
yet, it offers this once, after your first request.

## Train an agent

Press ⌘K and choose **Train <agent> by interview…**, or tell your Chief of Staff "train Finance by
interview". It asks what the job is, what good looks like, what the agent must never do, who it
works with, what it needs access to and when it runs. It then writes a job description into that
agent's instructions, merged with what's there rather than replacing it, and sets up any routines
you named. You can read and edit it any time in the agent's settings.
