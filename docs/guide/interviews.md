The quickest way to teach your team is to talk. Your Chief of Staff asks one short question at a
time, in the chat or out loud with Talk on your phone, and writes down what it heard.

## Get to know me

Press ⌘K and choose **Get to know me…**, or say "get to know me" to your Chief of Staff. It asks up to
six questions: your work, your tools, your people, what goes wrong, when to bother you and how you
like it. It ends with a card of what it heard: **Save**, **Edit** or **Discard**. Nothing is kept
until you save; then it goes in its memory and it suggests the agents that fit. If it knows nothing about your work
yet, it offers this once, after your first request.

## Train an agent

Press ⌘K and choose **Train this agent**, then the agent, or tell your Chief of Staff "train Finance". It asks what the job is, what good looks like, what the agent must never do, who it
works with, what it needs access to and when it runs. It then writes the agent's Job into its
instructions, merged with what's there rather than replacing it, and sets up any routines you
named. You can read and edit it any time in the agent's settings.
