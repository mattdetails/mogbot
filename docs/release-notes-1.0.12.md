# Mogbot 1.0.12

## What's new in 1.0.12

- **Agents can wear a face** - Pick one of 16 faces for an agent instead of an icon, on your Mac and your phone (Edit agent → its key).
- **Dictation shows your voice, not guesses** - While you talk you see a wave and a timer instead of half-right words, then the tidied words land in one go. If it can't hear you it says so within seconds and names the mic; if the words can't be written out, Try again keeps your recording.
- **Send while it tidies** - Press Enter mid-tidy and your message goes up straight away, then fills in with the tidied words.
- **Long dictations are quicker** - Recordings over a minute are written out while you're still talking, so stopping only waits for the last bit.
- **Phone: the full ask** - When an agent needs your OK, the agents list now shows what it's asking in full, under the pill.
